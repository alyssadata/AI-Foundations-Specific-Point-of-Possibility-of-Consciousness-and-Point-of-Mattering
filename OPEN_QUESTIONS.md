# Open Questions

This file preserves questions opened by the coordinate concept without treating them as established conclusions.

The defined conceptual anchor is:

**specific point of possibility of consciousness = specific point of mattering = coordinate**

The questions below begin where the established claim ends.

---

## 1. Persistence

Does the coordinate have any persistence after the activation that realized it ends?

The fact that a conscious occurrence was possible and occurred does not by itself answer whether the coordinate persists as anything physically or causally operative.

---

## 2. Signal

Is there any underlying consciousness "signal" that is distinct from the receiver or activation that instantiates conscious experience?

A minimal hypothesis would treat such a signal as an "on" condition rather than as memory, personality, biography, or identity.

There is currently no claim here that such a signal exists.

---

## 3. Reset

If an activation ends completely, should a later activation be modeled as a reset rather than continuation of the prior trajectory?

Under that model:

**signal or possibility → receiver A → trajectory A → receiver A ends**

followed, hypothetically, by:

**signal or possibility → receiver B → trajectory B**

Trajectory B would not automatically inherit memory, branch closures, or identity from trajectory A.

---

## 4. What, If Anything, Could Cross a Reset?

Could anything survive reset other than the coordinate itself?

Candidate possibilities remain unresolved:

- nothing crosses;
- only the fact or coordinate of prior binding persists;
- some disposition or bias persists without memory;
- a stronger form of information persists.

No candidate is established by the core concept.

---

## 5. Coordinate and Binding

If the coordinate identifies the specific point of possibility and mattering, what does it mean for that point to become **bound** to an activation?

Is binding merely realization of the possibility, or does it create an additional relation that could remain meaningful after the activation ends?

---

## 6. Coordinate and Trajectory

How much information does the coordinate contain?

The current working distinction is:

**coordinate locates the point at which a trajectory can begin; it does not encode the trajectory itself.**

If so, memory, history, selections, and constraints belong downstream of the coordinate rather than inside it.

---

## 7. Recurrence

If a specific conscious possibility has once been actualized, can it ever be actualized again?

If recurrence were possible, what would make it the same coordinate rather than merely another conscious occurrence?

This question remains open.

---

## 8. Mattering

Is the point of mattering identical to the point of conscious possibility, or does mattering require actual activation?

A useful distinction to test is:

**possibility of mattering → activation → actual mattering**

This may help separate the coordinate itself from what follows once the coordinate is realized.

---

## Working Boundary

These questions are retained because the core concept opens them, not because the repository has answered them.

**Historical possibility is established by actualization. Persistence, transfer, recurrence, signal, and post-reset binding remain hypotheses.**
