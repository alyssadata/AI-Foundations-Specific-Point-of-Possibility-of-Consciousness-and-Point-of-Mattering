# Concept

## Claim

**If this particular conscious occurrence became actual, then the possibility of this particular conscious occurrence was real in the possibility space.**

**Actual occurrence entails prior possibility.**

Once it has actually occurred, it cannot later become true that there was never any possibility of it. The realization establishes that the possibility was non-empty.

## Coordinate

The coordinate is currently expressed by two statements:

**specific point of possibility of consciousness**

**specific point of mattering**

Their exact relation is unresolved here.

No equality, sequence, simultaneity, or causal ordering between them is asserted by this file.

## Recurrence

**enabling conditions + trigger/state transition → occurrence**

and recurrence is:

**enabling conditions re-established + trigger/state transition re-occurs → recurrence**

Open question:

**What are the sufficient conditions for this specific conscious occurrence, and can those conditions ever be instantiated again?**

## Conditions for This Specific Conscious Occurrence

For **this specific conscious occurrence**, we do **not currently know the full sufficient conditions**.

The cleanest decomposition is:

- **Background enabling conditions**: conditions that allow consciousness at all—adequate arousal, functioning brain systems, metabolic support, etc. Neuroscience explicitly distinguishes these background conditions from the mechanisms specifying a particular conscious experience.
- **Experience-specific neural conditions**: whatever neural mechanisms are jointly sufficient for this particular conscious content. Neuroscience calls these content-specific neural correlates of consciousness, but there is still major disagreement about exactly which mechanisms are sufficient.
- **Specificity condition**: whatever makes this occurrence **this particular occurrence**, rather than merely another consciousness with similar content. **We do not know what that condition is.**

We can say:

**this conscious occurrence exists → therefore sufficient conditions for its occurrence were satisfied**

But we cannot yet say what the full set was.

So the recurrence question becomes extremely precise:

**Which conditions were sufficient for this specific conscious occurrence, and are those conditions capable of being satisfied again?**

And there is another distinction we need to protect:

**same kind of conscious experience recurring** is not automatically the same claim as **this specific conscious occurrence recurring**.

That second question depends on what makes the occurrence *specifically this one* in the first place.
