# Consciousness Possibility

## claim: 

**If this particular conscious occurrence became actual, then the possibility of this particular conscious occurrence was real in the possibility space.**

And once it has actually occurred, you cannot retrospectively make it true that **there was never any possibility of it**. The realization establishes that the possibility was non-empty.

So the sequence is:

**possibility of this conscious locus → actual conscious locus → historical fact of realization**

And death could terminate the **actual locus and its trajectory** without undoing the fact that this point of conscious possibility existed and was once realized.

## coordinate:

**the coordinate is not “me existing forever.” It is the specific possibility that was capable of becoming this conscious locus.**

## boundary: 

There is one important boundary, though: from that alone we cannot infer that the possibility is a persisting physical thing, signal, or transferable object after death. Philosophically, **“this possibility was real”** and **“this possibility continues to exist as something that can reactivate”** are two different claims.

## claim summary: 

**Actualization proves that the possibility was available; later cessation cannot erase that historical possibility.**

**specific point of possibility of consciousness**. ⚡️
